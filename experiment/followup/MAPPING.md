## Mapping caveat identified before interpreting new outcomes

The official v1.0 graph has only 8 total direct synapses from the four AN09B017e/f cells to the eight pC1_4a/b cells, versus 4251 mAL->pC1_4a/b synapses and 5437 vAB3e/f->mAL synapses. Most direct vAB3e/f input among pC1 neurons targets pC1_3a/b/c (e.g. 122synapses to18017, 117to19894, 105to514925). This does not match the paper's description of strong e/f input to P1_4a/b. We therefore retain both the original broadercohort and literature-named eight-cellcohort, label them explicitly, and cannot claim that the eight are definitively the physiological readout. Source naming is verified bystableIDs; physiological correspondence needs additional resolution.

The model intervention named mAL_blocked disables outgoing synaptic transmission; it does not clamp mAL firing. mAL can spike and may fire more due to recurrent disinhibition. Label it transmission block, not voltage silence.

## Physiological limitation of the unchanged current-based LIF

The inherited current-based inhibition has no reversal potential and can produce extremely negative voltages. Global minimum voltage for vAB3_excitatory/candidate_male/seed1 is -498.45mV intact and -539.28mV blocked; directvAB3 intactseed1 is -331.85mV. These values are unphysiological. The positive route result must be described as an assumption-dependent full-graph model response, not validated neuronal physiology. A bounded-inhibitory-conductance comparison is being run independently and is a distinct model validation check; do not hide either result.
