LOADING THE FLY
Model notes

A courtship circuit, visualized.

Fly movement and the brief mounting pose are illustrative animation. For this visual demonstration, a nonzero recorded P1 response makes a target eligible and the nearest eligible target is chosen; that rule is illustrative, not a validated courtship threshold or biomechanical simulation. The target nearest to the subject is male: this scene demonstrates male-directed courtship, not a reversal of mate preference.

The anatomical body is NeuroMechFly, derived from female micro-CT data; the scene uses it as an illustrative body for all three flies. Asset source · Kallman, Kim & Scott (2015)
